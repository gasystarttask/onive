# Dataset Licenses

This document tracks the licenses and usage policies for all datasets used in the project.

## License Summary

### CC0-1.0 (Public Domain)
- **OSCAR Malagasy Corpus**: No restrictions, public domain
- **Bible Parallel Corpus**: Public domain translations

### Various Open Licenses
- **OPUS Collection**: Multiple licenses depending on source corpus
  - Most are CC-BY or CC-BY-SA
  - Some may have more restrictive terms
  - Always check individual corpus documentation

## Usage Guidelines

### For Research Use
All datasets in this collection are suitable for:
- Academic research
- Non-commercial language model training
- Educational purposes

### For Commercial Use
Before commercial use, verify:
- Individual corpus licenses in OPUS collection
- Attribution requirements
- Share-alike obligations
