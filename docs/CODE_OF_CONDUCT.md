# Malagasy LLM (Onive) – Code of Conduct

This project follows a **Contributor Covenant**-style Code of Conduct to ensure a safe, respectful, and inclusive environment for everyone.  

---

## 🌍 Our Pledge

We, the maintainers, contributors, and community members, pledge to make participation in the **Onive** project a **harassment-free** experience for everyone, regardless of:

- Language or dialect  
- Technical skill level  
- Gender, gender identity, or expression  
- Age, body size, or appearance  
- Religion or beliefs  
- Nationality, ethnicity, or cultural background  

We commit to fostering an open and welcoming community.

---

## ✅ Our Standards

### Positive behaviors we encourage:
- Showing empathy and respect toward all contributors  
- Giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback  
- Focusing on collaboration rather than competition  
- Helping others grow and learn  
- Supporting diversity and inclusion in NLP and Malagasy language development

### Unacceptable behaviors:
- Harassment, hate speech, or discriminatory remarks  
- Trolling, personal attacks, or threats  
- Sharing harmful, violent, or unsafe data  
- Publishing others’ private information without consent  
- Using the project to create malicious applications  

---

## 🔄 Reporting Issues

If you witness or experience unacceptable behavior:  

- Open a **confidential report** via email: **gasy.starttask@gmail.com**  
- Or, file an issue on GitHub using the **“Code of Conduct Violation”** template.  

All reports will be reviewed promptly and kept strictly **confidential**.

---

## ⚖️ Enforcement

Project maintainers will:
1. Investigate reported issues quickly.  
2. Take appropriate corrective action, which may include:
   - Temporary or permanent bans  
   - Removal of offensive content  
   - Revocation of contributor privileges

Maintainers who fail to enforce this Code of Conduct may be removed from the project.

# Malagasy Translation 🇲🇬

## 🌍 Fanekena

Izahay, mpikarakara, mpandray anjara, ary mpikambana, dia manolo-tena hanangana tontolo **misokatra, tsy misy fanavakavahana** ao amin'ny tetikasa **Onive**, na inona na inona:

- Fiteny na dialekta  
- Haavon’ny fahaiza-manao ara-teknika  
- karaza
- Taona, bika aman’endrika  
- Fivavahana sy finoana  
- Foko, firenena, na fiaviana ara-kolontsaina  

---

## ✅ Fitsipika tokony harahina

### Fitondran-tena tsara:
- Manaja ny hafa sy mampiseho fahaiza-mihaino  
- Mamporisika ny fiaraha-miasa sy fizarana fahalalana  
- Manampy ny hafa hivoatra sy hianatra  
- Mampiroborobo ny fiteny Malagasy sy ny teknolojia misokatra  

### Fitondran-tena tsy ekena:
- Fanevatevana, fanavakavahana, na herisetra  
- Fandrahonana na fanenjehana manokana  
- Fampiasana angona mampidi-doza na tsy ara-dalàna  
- Fizarana vaovao manokana tsy nahazoana alalana  
- Fampiasana ny tetikasa ho amin’ny tanjona manimba
