# Safety Guidelines

## Medical Use
- This model is **not a medical authority**.  
- It should be used only by qualified practitioners to assist with **summarization and transcription**.  
- It must not be used as a substitute for medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment.  

## General Safety
- The model may produce hallucinations or inaccurate facts.  
- Users should always validate outputs before use.  
- Avoid deploying in high-stakes environments without human oversight.  

## Community Policy
- Contributions should respect Malagasy culture and language diversity.  
- Do not introduce unsafe, harmful, or discriminatory training data.
